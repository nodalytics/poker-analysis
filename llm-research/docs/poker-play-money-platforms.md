# Play-money and practice poker platforms

A list of places to play Hold'em without real money, collected 2026-11-05 as candidates for testing agents
outside our own PokerKit tables. **Not yet checked by us:** features, availability and terms below are as
collected and may be out of date. Check each one before using it.

## Before pointing an agent at any of these

- **Most poker rooms forbid bots and automated play, play-money tables included.** Their terms usually cover
  automated software of any kind, and accounts are closed for it. Do not run our agents on PokerStars, GGPoker,
  WSOP.com, partypoker, CoinPoker, Zynga, ClubWPT, Replay Poker or Poker with Friends unless their current terms
  allow it in writing.
- The safe targets are ones we run ourselves or that are built for bots: our PokerKit tables (`scripts/`),
  **PokerTH** run locally, and **Pip** offline against its own AI. Those also make good GUI tests: install PokerTH
  in the Windows VM and have an agent play it through Windows-MCP, graded from its logs.
- Private tables on Poker Now or Poker Patio are only for games where every player has agreed to bots.

## Major poker rooms with play-money lobbies

Same software as their real-money games.

- **PokerStars (Play Money):** a dedicated play-money section with many game types and tournaments.
- **GGPoker:** a play-money lobby on the same client as real money.
- **WSOP.com:** a play-money option on the World Series of Poker's platform.
- **partypoker:** a play-money lobby alongside real-money games.
- **CoinPoker:** a "Play For Fun" app with virtual chips for cash games and tournaments, no real-money registration.

## Browser-based practice and open-source tools

- **PokerTH:** free, open-source Texas Hold'em; play as a guest in the browser or offline.
- **Poker Now:** private browser games with friends, no download or registration, play money only.
- **Poker Patio:** free browser platform with public games and private tables.
- **nlh.poker:** free browser No-Limit Hold'em practice app, no download.
- **Pip:** open-source Hold'em against AI opponents, single player, free, no account.
- **QuantPlay:** free 6-max No-Limit Hold'em trainer in the browser.

## Social and app-based poker

Casual play with free chips and daily bonuses.

- **Zynga Poker:** one of the largest social poker games, large free-chip welcome bonus, social features.
- **ClubWPT (social poker):** the World Poker Tour's app; free Hold'em and Omaha, including tournaments, play chips.
- **Replay Poker:** between a social app and a browser tool; a website with play chips.
- **Poker with Friends:** free chips, daily rewards, private tables with friends.
