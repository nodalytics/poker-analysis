### Updated Skill File

#### Preflop
- **Tighten Up Further**: Play only top 0.05% of hands: pocket pairs 1111+, A-A, K-K. Fold all speculative hands, including A-Ks, unless you have a late position and the table is extremely loose. Focus on premium hands only.
- **Raise Size**: Open-raise to 13x the big blind. 3-bet aggressively against calling stations or loose players, but fold to re-raises unless you have pocket aces or kings. Fold to 3-bets from tight-aggressive players unless you have a monster hand.

#### Postflop
- **Aggression**: Bet 75% of the pot on strong hands with position. Fold to raises unless you have top pair or better. Play tighter against calling stations or nits, and more aggressive against tight-aggressive players. Bet for value and fold to any aggression unless you have a strong hand. Avoid bluffing unless the situation is highly favorable or you have a clear read.

#### New Additions
- **Table Dynamics**: Pay close attention to table aggression levels and adjust your strategy accordingly. Avoid playing speculative hands in tight games. Focus on value-betting and folding to aggression.
