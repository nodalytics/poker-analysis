### Updated Skill File

- **Maintain central control with pawns and pieces early.**
- **Castle early to secure the king and activate rooks.**
- **Avoid unnecessary king moves until castling is complete.**
- **Monitor opponent's king safety and exploit vulnerabilities.**
- **In defensive positions, prioritize solid pawn structure and limiting opponent's options.**
- **Stay adaptable and adjust strategy based on material imbalances.**
- **Focus on active piece play and concrete threats to avoid draws or stalemates.**
- **Avoid excessive piece exchanges in the opening unless they create a clear advantage.**
- **Be cautious of tactical traps and forcing moves that can lead to quick loss.**
- **In endgames, focus on king activity and exploiting weaknesses.**
- **Ensure piece coordination and avoid isolated weaknesses.**
- **Always assess opponent's threats before making a move.**
- **When facing material disadvantage, create threats and force exchanges to equalize.**

**MEMORY:** Avoid repetitive moves that do not lead to progress or material gain, as they can lead to time control issues or stalemate scenarios.
