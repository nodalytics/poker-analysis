### Updated Skill File

- **Develop knights and bishops early to control key squares and prepare for defense.**
- **Avoid unnecessary king moves in the opening; keep the king safe behind pawns.**
- **Be cautious of queen moves that leave the king vulnerable, especially when the opponent has active pieces.**
- **Always consider the opponent's potential threats before making a move.**
- **When defending, prioritize maintaining control of key squares and preventing the opponent's pieces from becoming active.**
- **Do not repeat moves unnecessarily; aim for progress and development in each turn.**
- **Be aware of material imbalances and adjust your strategy accordingly.**
- **When in a defensive position, focus on creating a solid pawn structure and limiting the opponent's attacking options.**
- **Avoid excessive repetition of moves that do not lead to progress; aim for dynamic play and active piece placement.**
- **Monitor the opponent's king safety and exploit vulnerabilities when possible.**
- **When facing a material disadvantage, focus on creating threats and forcing exchanges to equalize.**
- **Avoid perpetual checks unless they lead to a draw; aim for concrete progress instead.*
